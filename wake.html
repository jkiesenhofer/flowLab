Distance from bubble center:

\[ r = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2} \]

Velocity inside bubble:

\[ \mathbf{u}(x, y, t) = \begin{cases} 0, & r < a \\ \text{outside bubble (see below)}, & r \ge a \end{cases} \]

Base uniform flow:

\[ u_\text{base} = 0, \quad v_\text{base} = U \]

Deflection around bubble (potential-like flow):

\[ u_\text{deflect} = u_\text{base} \left(1 - \frac{a^2}{r^2}\right), \quad v_\text{deflect} = v_\text{base} - 2 \frac{a^2}{r^2} \frac{x y}{r^2} \]

Wake region (x > 0, turbulent perturbations):

\[ f_\text{decay}(y) = e^{-3 y^2} \]

\[ u_\text{wake} = 0.4 f_\text{decay}(y) \sin(5x - 4t) + 0.2 \sin(12 y + 6 t) \]

\[ v_\text{wake} = 0.6 f_\text{decay}(y) \cos(5x - 4t) + 0.2 \cos(12 x - 6 t) \]

Total velocity field:

\[ \mathbf{u}(x, y, t) = \begin{cases} 0, & r < a \\ \begin{bmatrix} u_\text{deflect} + u_\text{wake} \\ v_\text{deflect} + v_\text{wake} \end{bmatrix}, & r \ge a, x > 0 \\ \begin{bmatrix} u_\text{deflect} \\ v_\text{deflect} \end{bmatrix}, & r \ge a, x \le 0 \end{cases} \]
